25 ChatGPT Prompts Every Small Business Owner Should Bookmark

February 9, 2025 10 min read

Most people use ChatGPT wrong. They ask vague questions and get generic answers. These prompts are different — they're designed for specific business outcomes.

Copy them exactly, fill in the brackets, and watch your productivity jump.

💡 Pro Tip: Save these in a note-taking app (Notion, Apple Notes, whatever). Create a "ChatGPT Prompts" page and organize by category. You'll use these daily.

📧 CUSTOMER SERVICE (Prompts 1-5)

1. The Complaint Transformer

Copy This "I run [BUSINESS TYPE]. A customer just sent this complaint: [PASTE COMPLAINT] Draft a response that: 1. Acknowledges their frustration specifically 2. Explains what happened without making excuses 3. Offers a concrete solution 4. Sounds like a real person, not a corporation Keep it under 150 words."

2. The FAQ Generator

Copy This "Based on these 10 customer questions I received this week: [PASTE QUESTIONS] Create 5 FAQ entries that: - Address the real concern behind each question - Are written in a friendly but authoritative tone - Include a brief answer (2-3 sentences max) Format as Question → Answer pairs."

3. The Upsell Email

Copy This "Write a follow-up email to a customer who bought [PRODUCT] from me. Goals: - Thank them genuinely - Introduce [RELATED PRODUCT] as a natural next step - Explain why it complements what they already have - Include a soft CTA (not pushy) Tone: Helpful friend, not salesperson"

4. The Difficult Customer

Copy This "This customer keeps asking for unreasonable refunds/changes: [PASTE THREAD] Draft a response that: 1. Stands firm on my policy 2. Doesn't damage the relationship 3. Offers an alternative if possible 4. Is polite but clear I want to keep them as a customer if possible, but not at any cost."

5. The Review Request

Copy This "Write an email asking a happy customer to leave a Google review. Context: - They just told me they loved [SPECIFIC RESULT] - I want it to feel personal, not templated - Include a direct link to my review page - Make it easy (one click if possible) Keep it under 100 words."

📢 MARKETING (Prompts 6-12)

6. The Social Media Calendar

Copy This "I need a week's worth of social media posts for [PLATFORM]. My business: [WHAT YOU DO] My audience: [WHO THEY ARE] Goal: [ENGAGEMENT/SALES/AWARENESS] Create 7 posts (one per day): - Mix of educational, promotional, and personality - Include relevant hashtags - Keep captions under 150 words Format: Day 1: [content] Day 2: [content] etc."

7. The Headline Generator

Copy This "I wrote an article about [TOPIC]. Here are the key points: [BULLET POINTS] Generate 10 headline options that: - Speak to [TARGET AUDIENCE] - Promise a specific outcome or benefit - Use power words but avoid clickbait - Range from practical to curiosity-driven Include why each headline works."

8. The Sales Page

Copy This "Write a sales page for [PRODUCT/SERVICE]. Details: - Price: $[AMOUNT] - Target customer: [DESCRIPTION] - Main benefit: [OUTCOME] - Key features: [LIST] Structure: 1. Hook (problem they face) 2. Agitation (why it matters) 3. Solution (your offering) 4. Proof (testimonials/results) 5. Offer (what they get) 6. Guarantee (risk reversal) 7. CTA (what to do next) Tone: Conversational, confident, not hype-y"

9. The Email Subject Lines

Copy This "I need subject lines for an email about [TOPIC]. Generate 15 options across these categories: - Curiosity gap (makes them want to open) - Direct benefit (what they'll gain) - Urgency (time-sensitive) - Question (engagement) - Personal (uses 'you') Each should be under 60 characters."

10. The Competitor Analysis

Copy This "Here's what my competitor offers: [DESCRIPTION] And here's what I offer: [YOUR OFFERING] Help me position against them by identifying: 1. Their likely weaknesses 2. My unique advantages 3. Messaging that highlights the differences 4. Who should choose me vs. them Be honest—when should someone pick them instead?"

11. The Lead Magnet Ideas

Copy This "I need lead magnet ideas for [BUSINESS TYPE] targeting [AUDIENCE]. Generate 10 ideas that: - Solve a specific, urgent problem - Can be consumed in under 15 minutes - Showcase my expertise - Naturally lead to my paid offering For each, include: - Title - Format (checklist, template, guide, etc.) - Why it would work"

12. The Testimonial Request

Copy This "Draft an email asking a client for a testimonial. Context: - They achieved [SPECIFIC RESULT] - I want it to feel easy, not burdensome - I'll offer to draft something they can edit - Keep it short and casual Include suggested questions that would get a great testimonial."

⚙️ OPERATIONS (Prompts 13-18)

13. The SOP Creator

Copy This "I need an SOP (Standard Operating Procedure) for [TASK]. Here's how I currently do it: [DESCRIBE PROCESS] Create a document that: 1. Lists required tools/materials 2. Breaks down into numbered steps 3. Includes common mistakes to avoid 4. Has a quality checklist at the end 5. Estimates time needed Format so a new employee could follow it without asking questions."

14. The Meeting Agenda

Copy This "Create an agenda for a meeting about [TOPIC] with [ATTENDEES]. Goals: [WHAT YOU WANT TO ACCOMPLISH] Time available: [DURATION] Include: - Specific discussion points - Time allocation for each - Pre-meeting prep needed - Desired outcomes - Next steps format Keep total time under [DURATION]."

15. The Job Description

Copy This "Write a job description for a [ROLE] at my [BUSINESS TYPE]. Key responsibilities: [LIST] Required skills: [LIST] Nice-to-have: [LIST] Salary range: $[RANGE] Location: [REMOTE/ONSITE/HYBRID] Tone: Professional but approachable. We want someone who's competent but also a culture fit."

16. The Vendor Comparison

Copy This "I'm choosing between these vendors for [SERVICE/PRODUCT]: Option A: [DETAILS + PRICE] Option B: [DETAILS + PRICE] Option C: [DETAILS + PRICE] My priorities: [WHAT MATTERS MOST] Create a comparison that helps me decide, including: - Best overall value - Best if budget is tight - Best for [SPECIFIC USE CASE] - Red flags for each"

17. The Process Improvement

Copy This "Here's a process that takes too long: [DESCRIBE] It currently takes [TIME]. I want to get it under [GOAL TIME]. Analyze and suggest: 1. Steps that could be eliminated 2. Steps that could be automated 3. Tools that might help 4. Reordering that would make it faster Be ruthless—challenge whether each step is actually necessary."
㱈>18. The Cash Flow Forecast
Copy This "Help me create a 3-month cash flow projection. Current situation: - Monthly revenue (average): $[AMOUNT] - Monthly expenses: $[AMOUNT] - Big upcoming expenses: [LIST WITH DATES] - Expected revenue changes: [DESCRIPTION] Show me month by month: - Starting balance - Expected income - Expected expenses - Ending balance - Any red flags to watch for"

💡 STRATEGY (Prompts 19-25)

19. The Business Model Canvas

Copy This "Help me think through my business model for [BUSINESS IDEA]. What I know: - Product/service: [DESCRIPTION] - Target customer: [DESCRIPTION] - Price point: $[AMOUNT] Fill out a simplified business model canvas covering: - Value proposition - Customer segments - Revenue streams - Key activities - Cost structure - Potential risks Flag any gaps in my thinking."

20. The Pivot Analysis

Copy This "My business currently does [CURRENT OFFERING] but I'm considering pivoting to [NEW DIRECTION]. Help me evaluate: 1. What's working in my current model 2. What's not working 3. Evidence that the new direction would work better 4. Risks of switching 5. Minimum viable test to validate the pivot 6. Timeline for deciding whether to commit Be honest about whether I'm chasing a shiny object or solving a real problem."

21. The Pricing Strategy

Copy This "I'm pricing [PRODUCT/SERVICE]. My costs are approximately $[AMOUNT] per unit. Competitors charge between $[LOW] and $[HIGH]. My target market is [DESCRIPTION]. Help me think through: - Cost-plus pricing vs. value-based pricing - Positioning (premium, mid-market, budget) - Pricing tiers that might work - When to raise prices - Discounting strategy Include what I'd need to believe for each price point to make sense."

22. The Customer Interview Guide

Copy This "I want to interview my best customers to understand why they bought. Create an interview script that: - Makes them comfortable sharing honestly - Uncovers the real reasons they chose me - Reveals what almost made them not buy - Identifies what else they wish I offered - Finds out how they'd describe me to a friend Include follow-up questions to dig deeper when I get surface-level answers."

23. The Exit Plan

Copy This "I run [BUSINESS TYPE] doing approximately $[REVENUE]/year in revenue. I want to be able to sell it in [TIMELINE] years. What should I be doing NOW to maximize value later? Cover: - What buyers actually look for - Systems/processes that increase valuation - Red flags that kill deals - Timeline for preparation - Whether my current trajectory is on track Be realistic about what my business is likely worth today vs. what it could be worth."
㱈>24. The Delegation Planner
Copy This "Here are all the things I do in my business: [TASK LIST] Help me decide what to delegate first by analyzing each task for: - Hourly value (what it's worth to the business) - Delegation difficulty (how hard to train someone) - Risk if done wrong - My personal enjoyment of the task Create a priority order for what I should hand off, starting with the easiest wins."

25. The Weekly Review

Copy This "I want to do a weekly business review. Here's what happened this week: Wins: [LIST] Challenges: [LIST] Numbers: [KEY METRICS] Help me analyze: 1. What I should keep doing 2. What I should stop doing 3. What I should start doing 4. The one thing that would make next week successful 5. Any patterns I'm missing Keep me accountable—call out any excuses or rationalizations."

How to Use These Effectively

The secret isn't having prompts—it's using them consistently.

Pick 3-5 that match your biggest current pain points. Save them where you'll actually use them. Then build the habit of reaching for these before starting from scratch.

Within a week, you'll find yourself adapting them naturally. Within a month, you'll have your own library of go-to prompts customized to your business.

🎯 Challenge: Use at least one prompt from this list every day for the next week. Track which ones save you the most time—those are your winners.
